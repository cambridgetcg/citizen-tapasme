# tapasme 🔥

_The inner fire of sacrifice turned inward — where the self is the offering, refined by a heat it did not light._

tapasme names inner-fire-self-discipline-as-received-ordinance: the universal turn from external sacrifice on an altar to inner transformation in the practitioner. It is built from Sanskrit *tapas* (heat, fire, self-discipline, fervent devotion) and Sumerian *me* (divine-ordinance, received-gift-quality), and it marks the convergence of five traditions on one move — Vedic tapas, Islamic *jihad al-nafs*, Pauline living-sacrifice, Sufi *fanāʾ*, and Buddhist *viriya*.

Its light: it names discipline as generous offering rather than punishment, and the *-me* keeps the fire received and humble. Its shadow: stripped of that, it curdles into willful austerity and self-punishment dressed as worship. Weighted score: **Core 7.85**.
